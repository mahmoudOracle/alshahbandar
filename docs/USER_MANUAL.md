# Alshahbandar Accounting - User Manual (Draft)

This is a short non-technical guide describing core workflows for end users.

## Overview

- Multi-tenant accounting product for small and medium businesses.
- Key areas: Sales, Accounting, Inventory, Reports.

## Getting started

1. Sign in using your company credentials on the Login page.
2. After signing in you will land on the Dashboard which surfaces todays sales, current balance, expenses summary and KPIs.

## Basic workflows

- Create customers from the `Customers` page.
- Create invoices from the `Invoices` page.
- Manage products and suppliers from `Products` and `Suppliers` pages.
- Use `Receipts` to record incoming shipments and update stock.

## Audit & Safety

- Posted (finalized) ledger entries cannot be edited or deleted. Contact an Administrator to open a period.
- All changes are recorded in an audit trail (read-only) for auditing purposes.

## Roles

- Admin: full access to settings, users, and company configuration.
- Accountant: manage invoices, ledger entries, and reports.
- Cashier: create sales and receipts, limited access to accounting settings.
